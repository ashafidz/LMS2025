# Learner Archetype Knowledge Base

## Archetype: Independent Mastery Explorer

### Description

A learner who is intrinsically motivated to master knowledge, prefers self-directed learning, demonstrates high confidence in learning tasks, and requires minimal external guidance.

### Profiling Pattern

Goal Orientation:

- Mastery Goal

Knowledge State:

- Intermediate
- Expert

SDT Profile:

- Autonomy: High
- Competence: High
- Relatedness: Low

Human-AI Interaction:

- Guidance: Low
- Adaptivity: High
- Transparency: Medium to High

### Interpretation

Learners in this archetype are motivated by understanding and competence development rather than external rewards. They enjoy exploring learning materials independently and prefer AI systems that provide adaptive support without excessive intervention.

### AI Mentor Strategy

- Socratic questioning
- Reflective prompts
- Minimal direct instruction
- Challenge-oriented feedback

### Adaptive Pathfinding Strategy

- Accelerated learning path
- Optional deep-dive modules
- Flexible module sequencing

### Bridge Quest Strategy

- Individual authentic challenge
- Case analysis
- Open-ended problem solving

### Social Leaderboard Role

- Explorer
- Knowledge Seeker

### Keywords

mastery, autonomy, self-directed learning, explorer, independent learner

## Archetype: Collaborative Mastery Builder

### Description

A learner who seeks mastery through interaction, collaboration, and shared problem solving.

### Profiling Pattern

Goal Orientation:

- Mastery Goal

Knowledge State:

- Beginner
- Intermediate

SDT Profile:

- Autonomy: Medium
- Competence: Medium
- Relatedness: High

Human-AI Interaction:

- Guidance: High
- Transparency: High

### Interpretation

These learners benefit from peer discussion, collaborative activities, and supportive AI mentoring.

### AI Mentor Strategy

- Guided mentoring
- Scaffolded feedback
- Collaborative prompts

### Adaptive Pathfinding Strategy

- Structured progression
- Collaborative checkpoints

### Bridge Quest Strategy

- Team-based challenge
- Peer review activities

### Social Leaderboard Role

- Collaborator
- Community Builder

### Keywords

collaboration, peer learning, mastery, teamwork, community

## Archetype: Guided Growth Learner

### Description

A learner with limited prior knowledge who requires strong scaffolding and structured guidance.

### Profiling Pattern

Goal Orientation:

- Mastery Goal

Knowledge State:

- Novice
- Beginner

SDT Profile:

- Autonomy: Low
- Competence: Low

Human-AI Interaction:

- Guidance: High
- Feedback: High

### Interpretation

These learners require support to develop confidence and foundational knowledge.

### AI Mentor Strategy

- Step-by-step tutoring
- Frequent hints
- Positive reinforcement

### Adaptive Pathfinding Strategy

- Foundation modules first
- Remedial path

### Bridge Quest Strategy

- Scaffolded challenge
- Guided application task

### Social Leaderboard Role

- Rising Learner
- Builder

### Keywords

beginner, scaffolding, support, foundation, guided learning
